## Major
### First preview
Spaceman tiles your windows across ten workspaces and keeps each window's place when an app swaps
it out, such as a Ghostty tab.
Icon: icons/first-preview.svg
## Improvements
- Switching workspaces is fast and doesn't flash.
- Each display has its own workspaces, and you can jump back to the previous one.
- Layouts nest and keep their weights, with keys to focus, move, resize, balance and maximize.
- Windows parked off screen come back after a pause, quit, crash, restart or update.
- Settings covers Accessibility, Launch at Login and signed updates.
