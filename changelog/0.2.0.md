## Major
### Settings window
Settings has five panes: General, Rules, Workspaces, Shortcuts and Activity. Rules shows a map of
your workspaces, so you can see where each app goes and change it there.
Icon: icons/settings-window.svg
### Workspace HUD
A small capsule at the bottom of the screen names the workspace you switched to and draws its
layout. It follows quick switches, never takes focus, and can be turned off from the menu bar.
Icon: icons/workspace-hud.svg
### Swipe between workspaces
A three-finger swipe left or right goes to the next or previous workspace on the display under the
pointer.
Icon: icons/swipe.svg
## Improvements
- A four-step setup tour opens on a fresh install and walks through granting Accessibility access.
- The menu bar menu lists each display's workspaces, and choosing one switches to it.
- Activity in Settings summarizes recent window arrangements and anything that went wrong.
- Workspace switches stay fast while other apps keep the disk busy.
- Spaceman runs as one process and starts faster.
## Fixes
- Shortcuts that macOS stops delivering are registered again, and the missed press still runs.
