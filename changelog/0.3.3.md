## Major
- Replaced Sparkle with a direct in-app updater backed by `anoma.ly/release/spaceman` (ETag polling, resumable downloads, quit-time installation after full window restoration, Stable/Dev channel picker, and download-only fallback for read-only bundles).
- Added Homebrew support (`brew install --cask anomalyco/tap/spaceman`) with automatic cask updates on stable releases.
- Added `Option+Shift+Tab` and `spaceman ctl move-workspace [next|prev|<display>]` to move the focused workspace across displays while persisting the assignment.
- Added `Option+Shift+Space` and `spaceman ctl float` to toggle floating on individual windows.

## Improvements
- Made the Workspace HUD interactive without stealing focus: hover to hold, click a workspace to switch, click the gear for Settings, and right-click an app icon to float or pin it.
- Eliminated main-thread LaunchServices stalls (up to 870 ms), 40 Hz frontmost polling, and 1 Hz idle wakeups; batched per-window Accessibility attribute reads into one IPC and sped up workspace switch reveal checks.
- Upgraded the CLI with `--help`/`--version` and atomic `spaceman config`, `spaceman rules`, and `spaceman workspaces` subcommands with `--json` output.
- Tightened Settings across all panes: merged HUD enable and style into one `Off | Icons | Layouts` control, hid granted Accessibility status, grouped Shortcuts into Workspaces and Windows & Layout, made Rules scrollable, and simplified Activity.
- Automatically stacks an adjacent pair temporarily when three windows' minimum widths exceed a display, joins new windows into the last focused split, supports two-axis diagonal corner drags, and reflows sibling tiles immediately on confirmed last-window closes.

## Fixes
- Fixed a launch crash on macOS 26 caused by KVO titlebar layout hooking in Rune.
- Fixed tiling staying paused on non-US keyboard layouts (such as Italian - Pro and German QWERTZ) that lack a plain backtick key, and surfaced runtime hotkey errors in Settings.
- Fixed a 250 ms Rune occlusion retry stall that delayed subsequent Workspace HUD reveals and skipped the entry animation.
- Fixed `Option+H/J/K/L` failing across asymmetric splits, dead-ending on floating windows, or stalling on rapid consecutive taps.
- Fixed workspace switches snapping back to an earlier workspace across empty workspaces or asynchronous focus lag.
- Ignored always-on-top floating overlays (`layer != 0`, such as Slack Huddles and PiP) and added a bounded backoff circuit breaker against Electron resize-fight flicker.
- Fixed eight stateful parking, display-reconnect, recovery, and focus-follow edge cases found via model-based testing with `tear`.
