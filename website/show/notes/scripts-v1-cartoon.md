# Three draft scripts

All three share the frame: one person's Mac, seen straight on. The cursor is his hand. The narrator is
Kit's voice in first person. The visitor starts it with ⌥1 (a tap on phones). Times are rough.

What the show has to do honestly, whichever script wins: windows tile when he switches, workspaces
switch with Option+number, a rule routes an app to its workspace, the bar at the bottom shows the
switch (the "Grow" track: small layouts with app icons, the current one larger, a marker that
stretches as it slides).

Invented apps used everywhere: **Jot** (notes), **Postbox** (mail), **Chatter** (chat), **Daybook**
(calendar), **Browsy** (browser), **Shell** (terminal), **Files**, and later lair-only apps:
**Henchr**, **Watchtower** (cameras), **Orgo** (org chart), **T-Minus** (countdown).

---

## Draft A: "Gentle productivity"

**Comic engine.** A soft self-help voice. The vocabulary of a productivity podcast ("a place for
everything", "boundaries", "I'm proud of me") never changes; what it is applied to does. The voice
climbs from small and sad to screaming, and the picture does the escalating first, so the voice is
always a step behind what we can read on screen. Second viewing rewards: the clock in the menu bar,
the notes' list items, the rule list.

**Ending.** A call from Mom at the peak. One keystroke, ⌥1, and the lair is gone: a calm grocery
list. Sweet voice. A whispered "Option four." Hard cut.

### Beats

**1. Mess (0:00–0:17).** Windows pop in, faster and faster, until the screen is a pile: `Untitled`,
`Untitled 2`, `notes`, `notes (1)`, `notes FINAL`, `notes FINAL actually`, `Postbox — 4,812 unread`,
`Browsy — 212 tabs`, `Screenshot 2026-11-19 at 3.12.07 PM`, `Recipes I'll make someday`,
`taxes??`. A yellow sticky lands on top: `organize notes??`. The cursor drifts, drags one window, and
drops it behind another. Menu bar clock: 9:02.

- [quiet, a little sad, close to the mic] "Hi. I have a lot of windows open."
- [small, defeated] "My notes are in here. Somewhere."
- [sigh] "I wrote a note to organize my notes. Now I can't find that one either."
- The sticky gets buried by `Untitled 48`.
- [timid, hopeful] "Someone told me to press Option one."

A ⌥1 keycap rises in the middle of the screen. The visitor can press it; after 2.5 s he does.

**2. Relief (0:17–0:38).** ⌥1. Every window flies to a tile or into the bar: Jot's windows tile on 1,
Chatter and Postbox shrink into workspace 2's little layout in the bar, Browsy into 3. The bar
shows five workspaces. A soft thunk per window.

- [exhales, stunned, quiet] "Oh."
- [soft wonder] "Everything has a place."
- ⌥2, ⌥3, ⌥1 with the bar following. [gently pleased] "Notes on one. Messages on two. The internet
  is on three, where it can't hurt me."
- Spaceman's Rules window: `Chatter → workspace 2`. A Chatter window opens on 1 and slides off to 2.
  [calm, proud] "And a rule: anything from Chatter goes to two. It can't interrupt me any more."
- The sticky reads `organize notes ✓`. [teary] "I found my notes."

**3. Competence (0:38–0:52).** Faster switches. Jot checks things off: `✓ taxes ✓ dentist ✓ reply to
Dana ✓ drink water ✓ call Mom`. Postbox: `Inbox 0`. Clock 9:14.

- [bright, confident] "I answer email now. In the same week."
- [smug] "I drink water on a schedule. It's on workspace five."
- [too pleased, small laugh] "I'm on top of everything. For the first time in my life, I'm on top."

The wallpaper dims a few percent on "on top".

**4. Ambition (0:52–1:06).** The note retitles itself: `Notes` → `Plan` → `The Plan`. Body: `1.
Organize notes ✓ 2. Organize desk ✓ 3. Organize the neighbourhood 4. Organize everyone`. Daybook:
`9:00 Standup · 9:30 Acquire the moon · 10:00 Dentist`. Rules: `Henchmen → workspace 4`,
`Doubts → ignore`.

- [quiet, a bit too calm] "So I made a plan. A small one."
- [matter-of-fact] "Nine o'clock, standup. Nine thirty, acquire the moon. Ten, dentist."
- [softly delighted] "Henchmen go on four. Doubts, I ignore. There's a rule for that."

**5. Corruption (1:06–1:30).** Switching speeds up, the palette turns red. ⌥4: a grid of henchmen
tiles. ⌥5: a camera wall (`CAM 03 neighbour's gnome`, `CAM 11 the moon`, `CAM 07 Dana's desk`). ⌥6:
an org chart with `ME` on top and every box under it. ⌥7: `T-Minus` counting down to `MOON`. The bar
runs out of numbers and grows letters, A to Z.

- [faster, gleeful] "Option four, henchmen! Option five, cameras!"
- [shouting, thrilled] "Option six! Everyone works for me! It's in the org chart!"
- [unhinged] "I needed more workspaces. I have twenty-six more! A through Z!"
- [screaming] "Every window has a place! And your place is beneath me!"
- [maniacal laugh]

**6. The cut (1:30–1:42).** At T-minus 3, a call: `Mom — FaceTime`. Everything freezes.

- [instantly sweet] "Oh! It's my mom."
- ⌥1: the lair vanishes in one frame. A calm grocery list. [warm, normal] "Hi Mom! No, nothing. Just
  organizing my notes."
- [whisper] "…Option four."

One frame of the lair, then black, then the calm download card: **Ten workspaces. Only one has to
look normal.**

## Draft B: "The rules"

**Comic engine.** Spaceman's real rule config is his diary. Every beat adds rules in real TOML-like
rows, and they read like a confession: `Chatter → 2`, `Mom → float`, `Doubts → ignore`,
`Conscience → float`, `The neighbourhood → 6`. The strict config validator becomes the straight man:
it rejects `workspace = "beneath me"`. Jokes are written to be read; the voice reads only some.

**Ending.** He finally tries to route the moon. A plain macOS-style sheet: "Spaceman can only move
windows. 'The Moon' is not a window." He argues with it, and loses.

### Beats

**1. Mess (0:00–0:16).** Same pile as A, with the sticky `organize notes??`.

- [sad, quiet] "This is my desktop. People say it's a cry for help."
- [small] "I just want to find my notes."
- [timid] "Apparently you press Option one."

**2. Relief (0:16–0:36).** ⌥1, tiles, the bar.

- [stunned exhale] "Oh. Oh, that's nice."
- Rules window opens. Row 1 types itself: `Chatter → workspace 2`. [calm, explaining] "You can
  write rules. Chatter goes to two."
- A Chatter window opens on 1 and slides to 2. [pleased] "See? Gone. Not gone. On two."
- [quiet joy] "There are my notes."

**3. Competence (0:36–0:50).** Rows 2–4: `Postbox → 2`, `Browsy → 3`, `Mom → float`.

- [confident] "Mail goes to two. The internet goes to three."
- [proud, light] "Mom floats. She doesn't like being tiled."
- [too pleased] "I'm so organized. I've organized things that don't need it."

**4. Ambition (0:50–1:06).** Rows 5–8: `Henchmen → 4`, `Doubts → ignore`, `Conscience → float`,
`Dave from accounting → ignore`.

- [calm, intimate] "I've added a few rules."
- [reading, pleased] "Henchmen, four. Doubts, ignore."
- [dismissive] "My conscience floats. It's still there. It's just not in the way."

**5. Corruption (1:06–1:30).** Rows fill fast, the list scrolls: `The neighbourhood → 6`, `The city
→ 7`, `The ocean → 8`, `Everyone → beneath me`. A red error: `rules[12]: workspace must be 1–10 or
A–Z`. He fixes it: `Everyone → Z`. Henchmen wall, cameras, org chart, countdown as in A.

- [faster, gleeful] "The neighbourhood, six! The city, seven! The ocean, eight!"
- [shouting] "Everyone goes beneath me!"
- [furious at the screen] "What do you mean, invalid?!"
- [manic] "Fine! Everyone goes to Z!"

**6. The cut (1:30–1:42).** Last row: `The Moon → 9`. A sheet drops: *Spaceman can only move windows.
"The Moon" is not a window.* He tries ⌥9 again and again; the sheet shakes each time, as macOS does
for a wrong password.

- [screaming] "Option nine! Option NINE!"
- [beat, then very small] "…It's not a window."

Hard cut. Calm download card: **Spaceman moves windows. Only windows.**

## Draft C: "You pressed it"

**Comic engine.** The fourth wall. The visitor is in the show from the first keystroke: the narrator
asks them to press keys, thanks them, then starts giving them orders, then blames them. The more the
visitor actually presses, the more the narrator treats them as a henchman. Ends by tiling the page
the visitor is reading.

**Ending.** He adds a rule for the visitor's browser: `Visitor → workspace 4`. The real page around
the show (the heading, the sound button, the caption) is pulled into tiles inside his screen. Then a
hard cut back to a normal page.

### Beats

**1. Mess (0:00–0:15).** The pile.

- [quiet, sad] "Oh. Hi. Sorry. I didn't know anyone was watching."
- [small] "I can't find anything. I can't find my notes."
- [timid] "Could you press Option one? I can't. My hands are… full of windows."

The visitor presses ⌥1 (he does it himself after 3 s: "It's okay. I'll do it.").

**2. Relief (0:15–0:36).** Tiles, the bar, the rule demo as in A.

- [stunned] "Oh. You did that. Thank you."
- "Try Option two." (the visitor can; it moves on after 2 s) "That's my messages. Option three is the
  internet."
- Rule demo. [calm] "Chatter goes to two. Every time. I don't even have to ask."

**3. Competence (0:36–0:50).**

- [confident] "We make a good team. Me, the windows, and you."
- [too pleased] "I've never had a team."

**4. Ambition (0:50–1:06).** The Plan, the calendar, the rule `Henchmen → 4`. A new row:
`Henchman #1 (visitor) → workspace 4`.

- [intimate] "I made you a workspace."
- [reasonable] "It's number four. It's very nice. There's a window."

**5. Corruption (1:06–1:30).** Henchmen wall: one tile labelled `you`, showing a cursor-shaped
silhouette. Cameras, org chart (`ME` → `you` → everyone else).

- [faster] "Option five! Press it! Faster!"
- [shouting] "You pressed every key I asked! Do you know what that makes you?"
- [screaming] "A HENCHMAN!"

**6. The cut (1:30–1:42).** `Visitor → workspace 4` executes. The heading above the show, the sound
button and the caption slide into tiles inside his screen.

- [calm, satisfied] "There. Now you have a place too."

Hard cut. The page comes back plain, with the calm download card: **Spaceman tiles your windows.
Not you.**
