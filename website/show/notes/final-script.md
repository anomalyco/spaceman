# Final script: "For your windows"

Straight tone (see `tone.md`). Built from draft F's structure and last shot, with draft D's drill,
minutes and hedge material; critiques in `critique-v2-sol.md` and `critique-v2-astra.md`.

## Why this one

- Both critics ranked F > D > E and proposed the same hybrid: F's structure and pull-back ending,
  D's character material, and none of E's software puns about people.
- The ending is a reversal of who is watching whom, not a zoom gag. It is seeded silently twice
  (the photo series he keeps of No. 14's hedge, and the lit window across the street) and it is
  never labelled as a twist: the outer window is just titled the way he titles his own.
- The card line is the critics' pick, "For your windows.", which reads as product copy and, after
  the last shot, as something else.
- He's American, so it's an HOA, trash cans and feet.

## Product truth

- ⌥1 is not a "tile everything" command. The visitor's ⌥1 starts the film: Spaceman's menu bar
  item and bar appear, then the windows on workspace 1 tile. We never say ⌥1 tiles.
- Moving and switching are shown separately: focus Postbox, ⌥⇧2 sends it away and 1 reflows; a
  beat later ⌥2 finds it there. The bar is enlarged for the section, and the keys show beside it.
- One rule is *made* on screen, field by field (App: Chatter, Action: Route, Workspace: 2), and a
  *new* Chatter window obeys it. Later the rules list has grown to a title rule
  (`Viewer, "No. 14 — Hedge" → 5`). First match wins; no Ignore row is shown.
- No float, no multi-display claim, no named workspaces.
